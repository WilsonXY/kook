# Recordings are paced for a person

**Context.** On WilsonXY/Bokli#74 the six attached recordings ran 2.4–6.5 s each. A whole flow (double tap, lost reply, retry) played at browser-driver speed, too fast for the owner to see what was being proved. `2026-10-06-ui-proof-attached.md` asked for a recording but said nothing about whether anyone could follow it.

**Decision.** `full-cycle` step 3: slow the recorded steps to a pace a person can follow, hold on each result that proves the change, and check the recording's length before attaching it.

**Rejected.** A fixed minimum length or a doctor check: the right pace depends on the flow, and the fix lives in each repo's flow files or proof script, which Kook can't check.
